Email delivery test: privacy

24 September 2026. This notice covers the invited pre-launch email delivery test only, not a general launch or newsletter subscription.

Who is responsible?

Frederico van Zeller, acting in his own name, is responsible for this test. Contact: info@rawstage.app.

What the test does

Entering your own email and submitting the form requests a confirmation email. Following its link and pressing the confirmation button requests the beta download email. Neither action subscribes you to announcements. We record requests and confirmation, not completed downloads or installations.

Google Workspace provides the email service and private request register. The register stores your address, request and confirmation timestamps, token expiry, a hash of the confirmation token, sending states and notice version. The purpose is to deliver the requested link, verify the delivery flow and prevent abusive sending. Our basis for handling these requests is our legitimate interest in operating and testing the beta-access service you requested. You may object by contacting us. Providing an address is necessary for the email test, not for visiting the website.

Your choice about Google reCAPTCHA

Google reCAPTCHA loads only after you press Allow. This permission is separate from your request for an email. It processes browser and interaction information, including your IP address, for security and abuse prevention. Google documents the _GRECAPTCHA risk-analysis cookie. The actual cookie inventory and lifetimes in this configuration are still being checked as part of this test; we do not claim that linked Google pages are cookie-free.

Google processes reCAPTCHA customer data on our behalf under the Cloud Data Processing Addendum. See its current service information. This test page has no Meta Pixel or Google Analytics integration.

You may refuse and email info@rawstage.app for a manual reply instead. The withdrawal button reloads the page without reloading reCAPTCHA unless you allow it again. This does not reverse prior processing or erase Google's cookies; you can remove cookies in browser settings. Your choice is kept only while this page is open, not in our own cookies or browser storage.

Retention and providers

Unconfirmed request records become due for removal after seven days; confirmed records after ninety days from confirmation. Removal occurs at the next successful cleanup. Daily cleanup has been scheduled and manual execution tested; its first scheduled run has not yet been verified. Removal from the active register does not remove email copies, provider backups or revision history. Contact us if you want the test correspondence deleted too.

GitHub Pages hosts this page and GitHub Releases hosts the download. Those services receive technical request information such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement. Google and GitHub may process information outside the EEA. No EU-only hosting claim is made. Contact us for information about the applicable processing and transfer arrangements. Provider-contract and retention checks remain part of the work required before the general launch.

Your rights

Subject to applicable conditions, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability and object to processing. You may withdraw permission without affecting earlier lawful processing. Contact info@rawstage.app; you may also complain to the CNPD or another competent supervisory authority. No automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects is made by this test.
