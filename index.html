One window · three rooms

Everything a session needs. Nothing it doesn't.

Cull

Keep the keepers. Lose the rest. Don't wait for either.

A grid that opens instantly and never stutters. Rate, flag and filter with one hand on the keyboard. No import, no catalog — point it at a folder and start.

Develop

The classic darkroom panel. Nothing you won't use.

White balance, exposure, curves, levels, color — the full traditional toolset, rendering as fast as you can move a slider. Every edit lives in a small sidecar file next to your RAW: portable, readable, yours.

Tether

Shoot to the screen. Keep shooting.

Plug in your camera and every frame lands in the session, develops with your session look, and is ready to judge in about a second. Unplug, replug, let the camera sleep — the session doesn't care.

Why another RAW app?

Because right now you're paying rent on one app that does too much, or juggling two that each do half. Session work needs exactly three things — capture, selection, development — and needs them fast, in one place, without a catalog database between you and your files.

We're building that. Nothing else.

  • One-time purchase. You buy it, you own it. Updates included for the version you bought.
  • No catalog. The folder is the session. Move it, archive it, open it in ten years — everything travels with the files.
  • No cloud. Your photographs never leave your machine.
  • macOS native. Built with Metal for Apple silicon — not a cross-platform port.
The beta, honestly

What's in the box today.

Works now

Native USB tether for Fujifilm X / GFX (tested end-to-end on a GFX100S — sleep, cable pulls, bursts). Hot-folder tether for any brand. Instant culling grid with keyboard ratings, flags, color tags and filters. The full classic develop panel: white balance with eyedropper, exposure to blacks, luma + RGB curves, levels, clarity, vignette, sharpening and noise reduction, single-pixel (hot pixel) removal, crop with corner rotation. Copy/paste settings across selections. Session look that follows your edits in tether. Export to JPEG, PNG, TIFF-16 and HEIC.

Not yet

Film-style LUTs and presets (coming to the Pro engine), masks, color wheel, dehaze. UI is English for now — Portuguese is on the way. Native tether is verified on GFX100S; other recent X / GFX bodies should work over the same protocol — that's part of what this beta is for. Beta builds expire after ~60 days; grab the newest one here.

What we'd love from you

Plug in your camera body and tell us if tether works — the model name is half the report. Run one real session end-to-end: shoot, cull, develop, export. If anything feels slow, stutters or surprises you, use Help → Send Feedback in the app — Help → Export Logs attaches the technical story in one file.
