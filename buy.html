Questions

Is this a subscription?
No. Pro is paid once, and the licence is yours.
How many Macs can I use it on?
Two at a time — say, the studio Mac and your laptop. You can move a licence to a new Mac from inside the app.
What if it's not for me?
Ask for a refund within 14 days, no questions. Better still, use RawStage Free on a real session first: it has no time limit.
Do I get updates?
Every 1.x update is included. When 2.0 arrives, licence holders upgrade at half price — free if you bought in the six months before it.
Do I need an account?
No. Your licence is a key. RawStage contacts our server once to activate it, then works offline.
Who handles the payment?
Paddle, our reseller and Merchant of Record. They take care of payment, VAT, invoices and refunds.
What does the founding price include?
