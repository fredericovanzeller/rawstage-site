Beta requests and downloads

You can download RawStage without registering or supplying an email address. If you separately volunteer as a tester, we use your email address with your consent to contact you about beta faults and request feedback during the 90-day registration period. These operational contacts must not include marketing or new-build announcements unless you separately subscribe to those announcements. Download access is independent of registration; no confirmation or delivery email is sent. The address is declared, not verified. Registration does not subscribe you to update announcements and does not prove that you own the address, completed a download or installed the app.

Existing testers can find the latest published build on the site without subscribing to emails. This is not an identity-enforced download system.

Google Workspace stores the private registration register: email address, registration time, deletion deadline, unverified-address status and notice version. It contains no confirmation token, marketing consent, download tracking or installation data. Visiting the separate optional Brevo form does not automatically send your address to it.

The basis for voluntary tester registration is your consent, given through a separate, initially unchecked option. You may withdraw at any time by emailing info@rawstage.app. Withdrawal stops tester contacts and prompts removal of the active registration, without affecting download access or the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. The maximum active retention period is 90 days.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, and downloads are hosted on GitHub Releases. Requests to those services may involve technical information such as your IP address, browser information and request logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Cookies and the anti-bot check

The new site's own code does not include Meta Pixel, Google Analytics or advertising and remarketing trackers. We do not use advertising cookies to identify who requested the beta.

The registration page offers a choice before loading Google reCAPTCHA. Allowing it loads Google's anti-bot service, which processes browser and interaction information, including your IP address, and uses the _GRECAPTCHA cookie for risk analysis. Google processes reCAPTCHA customer data on our behalf for security and abuse prevention under its Cloud Data Processing Addendum. Google changed this role on 2 April 2026; our notice, rather than acceptance of Google's general consumer terms, explains this processing.

You may refuse reCAPTCHA and download directly without registering. For help, email info@rawstage.app. Support receives a manual reply and does not subscribe you to announcements. Your choice is not saved in a cookie or browser storage. The withdrawal control reloads the request page without loading reCAPTCHA again unless you allow it. This does not undo data already sent to Google or erase Google's cookies; browser settings allow you to remove them.

Before publication: inspect cookies and their actual lifetimes during a real challenge, complete the provider-contract checks and record the consent/withdrawal procedure. Do not infer a cookie's lifetime from the two-minute validity of a challenge token. Linked Google and Brevo pages need their own inspection; the no-advertising statement above is not a claim that all external platforms are cookie-free.

How long information is kept

The new registration code is prepared to remove active records ninety days after registration. Repeated requests during that period do not extend it. This period applies to declared, unverified email addresses and was approved by the owner. The old email-request register remains separate: seven days for unconfirmed requests and ninety days from confirmation for confirmed requests. Old records are not relabelled as new registrations or silently imported into a newsletter. Cleanup for the new register is implemented locally on registration and in the daily maintenance handler; deployment and a successful scheduled execution must still be verified. Deletion occurs at the next successful cleanup after expiry. Removing an active row does not remove provider backups, revision history or email copies.

Proposals requiring approval and provider configuration: delete unconfirmed signup requests after 7 days; retain active subscriptions until withdrawal or the end of the beta newsletter; delete ordinary closed support correspondence after 12 months. Define separately a justified minimum record and retention period for consent evidence, suppression of further messages and any legal claims. These periods are not yet implemented.
