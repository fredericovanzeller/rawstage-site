Privacy

Last updated 2 November 2026. The previous version is dated 27 October 2026.

The short version. RawStage is local-first. Your photographs, edits and sessions stay on your Mac; the app has no account, no analytics and no telemetry. This website sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics; the one page that loads anything from a third party is the buy page, which loads Paddle's checkout. We handle personal data in four cases: when you buy a Pro licence (through Paddle, our reseller), when the app activates and checks that licence, when you apply to the tester programme, and when you write to us.

Who is responsible

Frederico van Zeller, who develops and sells RawStage in his own name (not through a company), is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: info@rawstage.app.

The app

  • Photographs, edits and sessions never leave your Mac. Edits are saved as small files next to your RAWs, in the folders you choose.
  • Update checks. RawStage asks rawstage.app/appcast.xml — or appcast-beta.xml, in a tester build — whether a newer build exists (using the open-source Sparkle updater). Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and basic technical details such as the app version to the site's host. It sends no profile of your Mac and nothing about your photographs.
  • Feedback is always your choice. Help → Send Feedback… and Help → Export Logs… prepare an email or a file on your Mac; nothing is sent until you send it yourself. The diagnostic log describes what the app did — it can include the names of your photo files, your Mac model and macOS version. Before the log is exported, the path of your home folder and the names of your session folders are removed from it (builds up to 23 exported them as they were). You can read it, and remove anything, before sending. Feedback sent from the app goes to info@rawstage.app (builds up to 23 sent it to the developer's Apple iCloud Mail address).

Buying a licence

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders: it takes the payment, issues the invoice, handles VAT and sales tax, and processes refunds. At checkout Paddle collects what a sale needs — your name, email address, country and billing address, payment details and, for businesses, a VAT number — under Paddle's privacy policy. We never see your card or bank details.

From Paddle we receive your email address, the identifiers of your order, the product, currency and amounts, and your country. We keep them in our licence database, hosted on Cloudflare, so that we can issue your licence key, tell which Macs it is active on, and act on refunds and chargebacks. We keep these records for as long as your licence exists, and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

The emails this needs — your licence key, and any notice about your order — are sent from info@rawstage.app through Brevo, an email service based in France. They are transactional: no newsletter is attached to a purchase. They are plain text, with no images and no links, so they carry no open-tracking pixel and no tracked links; we do not know whether you opened them. Brevo keeps the delivery log (address, time, delivered or bounced) under Brevo's privacy policy.

Activating and checking a licence

When you enter a licence key, RawStage sends our licence server the key, the app version and an identifier of your Mac. That identifier is a one-way hash (SHA-256) of the Mac's hardware UUID with a fixed suffix: it tells our server "the same Mac again" and nothing else, and the UUID itself never leaves your Mac. The server answers with a signed token that the app stores in a file only your user account can read. A Pro licence can be active on two Macs at a time; deactivating a Mac tells the server to free it.

Afterwards the app checks the licence against the server from time to time — at most once every seven days. Each check sends the token, which carries the licence id and the same hashed identifier. The check never blocks the app: without a network connection the last token stands, and the licence works offline indefinitely.

The licence server runs on Cloudflare Workers. Like any web request, a call to it reveals your IP address to Cloudflare; we keep an audit trail of licence events (activations, deactivations, payments, refunds) for as long as the licence records above. Nothing about your photographs, your edits or how you use the app is sent — there is still no analytics and no telemetry.

The tester programme

If you apply to RawStage Testers, the application asks for your email address, your camera bodies, the kind of work you do, how much of it you shoot tethered, and the Mac you use. We use these answers only to run the programme: to choose whom to invite, to send early builds through the tester update channel, and to write to you about them. Your name appears in the app's credits only if you say so. Applications are received by email at info@rawstage.app. If you are not selected we delete your application after 90 days; if you are, we keep it for as long as you are in the programme, or until you ask us to delete it.

This website

rawstage.app is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the downloads on GitHub Releases. As the host, GitHub receives technical request data such as your IP address and browser information; see GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and loads no advertising pixels or embedded fonts; downloading RawStage requires no email address or registration.

One small thing the site does keep in your browser: if you arrived through a link we sent out that carries a ?ref= tag — the name of a publication, a newsletter or a video, so we know which of them reached you — that tag is remembered for the rest of the tab (sessionStorage, gone when you close it) and sent with your order if you buy. It says nothing about you, it is never shared with anyone but Paddle as part of the order, and nothing is stored for a visitor who arrives without such a link.

The buy page is the exception. rawstage.app/buy loads one third-party script, Paddle.js, from Paddle's servers, and opens Paddle's checkout as an overlay on the page. Paddle's checkout sets its own cookies and browser storage to run the purchase and to detect fraud, under Paddle's privacy policy; it loads only when you open rawstage.app/buy, not on any other page. The buy page also asks our licence server for the current prices — an anonymous request that, like any web request, reveals your IP address to Cloudflare, which hosts the server. If you arrive on the buy page through a personal purchase link we sent you, the link carries the identifier of your order, and Paddle opens that order.

When you email us

If you write to info@rawstage.app, or send feedback from the app, we use your address and message only to reply and to fix what you report. The info@rawstage.app mailbox is hosted by Google Workspace. We keep support correspondence for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Email addresses collected before 25 October 2026

Until 25 October 2026, the download page asked for an email address to unlock the download, and said it would be used for news of new builds. Those submissions were processed by Formspree, a form service based in the United States, and forwarded to our mailbox — so the page's statement that addresses went "nowhere else" was not accurate, and we have since removed that form.

We will not use those addresses for marketing or add them to any mailing list — not even to announce the founding price. You can ask us to delete yours at any time, and we will delete all of them by 31 January 2027.

Legal bases and international processing

We process purchase and licence data because it is needed to sell you the licence and keep it working (the contract with you), and afterwards because tax and accounting law requires us to keep records of sales. We reply to your messages because you asked us to (and in our legitimate interest in supporting the software). Tester applications, and your name in the credits, rest on your consent, which you can withdraw. Addresses given on the old download form were provided with your consent, which you can withdraw. Hosting and security data is processed in the legitimate interest of running and securing the site and the licence server.

Paddle (United Kingdom and United States), Cloudflare, GitHub, Google, Apple and Formspree may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States, under the transfer safeguards described in their own privacy terms. Brevo processes the transactional emails in the European Union.

In short, the third parties that handle data on our behalf are: Paddle (the purchase and the checkout), Cloudflare (the licence server and its database), Brevo (the transactional emails), GitHub (this website and the downloads) and Google (the info@rawstage.app mailbox). The app's update check (Sparkle) reaches only our site.

Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Where processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to info@rawstage.app. Deleting licence records while a licence is active would end that licence; we will tell you before we do it. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) or the authority where you live.

Changes

If RawStage starts handling personal data in a new way, this page will be updated first, and anything that needs your consent will ask for it separately.
