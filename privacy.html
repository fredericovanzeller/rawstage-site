Privacy

Last updated 25 October 2026.

The short version. RawStage is local-first. Your photographs, edits and sessions stay on your Mac; the app has no account, no analytics and no telemetry. This website sets no cookies and loads no trackers. We only handle personal data when you write to us — and, until 25 October 2026, when the download page asked for your email address.

Who is responsible

Frederico van Zeller, who develops RawStage in his own name (not through a company), is responsible for the personal data described here. Contact: info@rawstage.app.

The app

  • Photographs, edits and sessions never leave your Mac. Edits are saved as small files next to your RAWs, in the folders you choose.
  • Update checks. RawStage asks rawstage.app/appcast.xml whether a newer build exists (using the open-source Sparkle updater). Like any web request, this reveals your IP address and basic technical details such as the app version to the site's host. It sends no profile of your Mac and nothing about your photographs.
  • Feedback is always your choice. Help → Send Feedback… and Help → Export Logs… prepare an email or a file on your Mac; nothing is sent until you send it yourself. The diagnostic log describes what the app did — it can include file and folder names, your Mac model and macOS version. You can read it, and remove anything, before sending. Feedback sent from the app arrives in the developer's Apple iCloud Mail.

This website

rawstage.app is hosted on GitHub Pages, and the downloads on GitHub Releases. As the host, GitHub receives technical request data such as your IP address and browser information; see GitHub's privacy statement. The site itself uses no cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, embedded fonts or third-party scripts, and downloading RawStage requires no email address or registration.

When you email us

If you write to info@rawstage.app, or send feedback from the app, we use your address and message only to reply and to fix what you report. The info@rawstage.app mailbox is hosted by Google Workspace. We keep support correspondence for up to 12 months after the conversation ends, unless you ask us to delete it sooner.

Email addresses collected before 25 October 2026

Until 25 October 2026, the download page asked for an email address to unlock the download, and said it would be used for news of new builds. Those submissions were processed by Formspree, a form service based in the United States, and forwarded to our mailbox — so the page's statement that addresses went "nowhere else" was not accurate, and we have since removed that form.

We will not use those addresses for marketing or add them to any mailing list. You can ask us to delete yours at any time, and we will delete all of them by 31 January 2027, when the current beta ends.

Legal bases and international processing

We reply to your messages because you asked us to (and in our legitimate interest in supporting the software). Addresses given on the old download form were provided with your consent, which you can withdraw. Hosting data is processed in the legitimate interest of running and securing the site.

GitHub, Google, Apple and Formspree may process data outside the European Union, including in the United States, under the transfer safeguards described in their own privacy terms.

Your rights

You may ask for access to, correction or deletion of your personal data, ask us to restrict or stop processing it, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Where processing relies on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time. Write to info@rawstage.app. You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority (CNPD) or the authority where you live.

Changes

If RawStage starts handling personal data in a new way — for example an optional newsletter or paid licences — this page will be updated first, and anything that needs your consent will ask for it separately.
